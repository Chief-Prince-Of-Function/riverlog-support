RiverLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 21, 2026

RiverLog respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information You Provide

RiverLog stores information you choose to enter, such as trip logs, catch details, gear information, notes, and photos.

How Information Is Used

Your information is used to provide core app functionality, including tracking trips, logging catches, managing gear, and generating insights.

Photos and Device Permissions

RiverLog may request access to your device camera or photo library to allow you to capture or attach images to your entries. Access is only used for this purpose.

Data Storage

Data may be stored locally on your device. If future features include cloud storage or syncing, data may be securely transmitted and stored using supported services.

Data Sharing

RiverLog does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Control

You control the information you enter into RiverLog. You may stop using the app at any time.

Children’s Privacy

RiverLog is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: riverlogapplication@gmail.com
